Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Core Statement: BorderCraft values your privacy. We promise not to actively collect, store, or share any of your personal identification information with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

BorderCraft is designed with minimalism in mind and does not collect any personal data (such as your name, phone number, email, or precise location).

2. Apple Official Services

To ensure proper game operation and core features, this game only uses standard official services provided by Apple Inc.:

  • Apple Game Center: Used for achievements. Related data is securely managed by Apple's official system; developers cannot access your private account information.
  • iCloud Save Sync: Used to securely synchronize your game progress to your personal iCloud space. This data is entirely controlled by you; developers cannot view, collect, or access your cloud saves.

3. Third-Party SDKs

This game does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, advertising, or social sharing SDKs, ensuring maximum privacy and a pure experience.

4. Children's Privacy

Since this game does not collect any personal data, it is safe for players of all ages.
